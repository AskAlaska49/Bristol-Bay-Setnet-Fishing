About Setnet Fishing

Setnet fishing in Bristol Bay, Egegik, Alaska is an amazing opportunity to learn and experience the excitement and thrill of commercial fishing. This includes learning different knots, working together as a team, supporting each other, working hard, quickness skills, reliability, long hours, quality workmanship, be respectful and treat equipment and supplies with care, following directions, being punctual, and so much more. No drugs or alcohol allowed for your safety and the safety of others.

Our Team

We are looking for people who are willing to work hard, learn, and be a part of a team. We are looking for people who are willing to work long hours, be quick, and work together as a team. We are looking for people who are willing to learn different knots used in setnet fishing, treat equipment and supplies with care, and so much more. Everyone is part of the team, everyone has a job to do, everyone is respectful, kind, helpful, and work together.

Setup Beach Gear

At the beginning of the season, here are some of the things that need to be done: Totes are moved around and sorted to which running lines, signs are put on the bluff behind each site, ropes (hall back line, 2-safeties, 2-block ropes, extra rope line) needed for each fishing nets site. 2-Blocks mounted on boards, screw anchors, 4-buoys (3 orange, 1 white), snatch block (block used to pull lines in and out for the net-the pin is never set on the ground, it is always put back in the slot), spreader bars, corks lines, are set up. Next, the running line is pulled from the up river post through the outside screw anchors block, to the side steak block and back to shore to the down river post. Extra line is figured 8 high on bank in two piles and then flipped over so the rope feeds from the top and 2 large rocks are placed on the ropes so the tide doesn't take them out. The snatch block is hung on the post with the pin locked. Spreader bars are tied together by upriver post.

Fishing

Fishing with the nets, boats, income and out go tide on beach. These are general ideas of procedures. Setting up the net and set time: The cork line is tied to the upriver part of the spreader bar where the orange buoy is attached. Corks are always flaked to the upriver side and lead to the downriver side. The lead line is attached to the downriver part of the spreader bar. Spreader bars are attached together with net between and released when time to set. Boat and Picking from Boat: Everyone who is in the boat MUST have a life jacket on, correctly fitted, and buckled before you get on the boat. Life jackets need to be worn under rain coat/gear to prevent from catching on net.

Packing Up for the Season

Lower and Upper cabins are cleaned, items picked up and put away, crates are packed and taken across.

Suggested Items to Bring / Clothing / Footwear

Some suggested items to bring are: 5/6 hoodies (some heavy, some light), 4/5 long sleeve shirts, 4/5 T-shirt, 1/2 set of long underwear, 2 pairs of jeans, 5/6 pairs of socks, 3/4 pairs of thick socks, tennis shoes, slip on shoes (like crocks), 7/8 underwear, 6/8 sweatpants (some light and some heavy), warm jacket, toiletries, personal items

We Provide

Some of the things we provide are: housing, bedding, meals (Some examples throughout the season: pasta dishes, pancakes, eggs, soups, meats, casseroles, fish, muffins, brownies, etc.), fishing gear (neoprene, bibs, boots, gloves, rain jacket, life jackets), towels, head lamp, head net.

Other Information

The weather can be unpredictable. Some days may be warm and sunny, while others may be cold and rainy. We even got a little wet snow one morning! Laundry is done about every 3 to 4 weeks, depending on the fishing. Showers are available at a variety of times, depending on the fishing. There is a generator that will run at various times to charge radios, etc. The drinking water comes from a well and is put in the water tower for drinking. Other water is pumped from the "drinking lake" and goes through several filters. We store well water in jugs for drinking until the water is set up. Food is purchased for the crew for the summer. You are able to have "gift" packages sent to you if you would like. All food kept in the upper cabin due to the wildlife. Each person will sign a contract and be responsible for their own actions as stated in the contract. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us and be sure to read over your signed contract.
