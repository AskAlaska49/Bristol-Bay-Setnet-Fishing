About Setnet Fishing

Setnet fishing in Bristol Bay, Egegik, Alaska is an amazing opportunity to learn and experience the excitement and thrill of commercial fishing. This includes learning different knots used, working together as a team, supporting each other, working hard, quickness skills, realizability, long hours, quietly workmanship, be respectful and treat equipment and supplies with care, following directions, being punctual, and so much more.
